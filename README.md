##  Overview

**Aero-Dash 2026** is the flagship annual competitive design and engineering event for first-year undergraduate students at IIT Mandi. The competition provides a structured, hands-on introduction to fundamental aerodynamics, structural design, payload distribution, and iterative engineering.

Participants design, construct, and fly unpowered gliders using standardized lightweight materials provided by the organizing committee. The event tests pure glide performance (distance & time) in **Round 1**, followed by payload-carrying capacity under the **"Nut Ladder" challenge** in **Round 2**.

> *"Lift is just gravity's way of saying 'try again in five minutes.'"*  
> — Anonymous Fresher, Round 1

---

##  Objectives

- **Aerodynamic Mastery:** Understand and apply principles of lift, drag, thrust, weight, aerofoil selection, and static stability.
- **Design Trade-offs:** Balance wing design between maximum unweighted flight efficiency (**Round 1**) and structural stability under heavy payloads (**Round 2**).
- **Hands-on Prototyping:** Foster teamwork, technical communication, and rapid fabrication skills under real-time constraints.

##  Schedule & Event Structure

The event takes place over a single intensive day divided into five phases:

| Phase | Duration | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 1: Orientation** | 45 Mins | Aerodynamic principles, launch safety, and live demo build. |
| **Phase 2: Fabrication** | 2.5 Hours | Team design and construction in the workshop area. |
| **Phase 3: Trial Testing** | 30 Mins | Supervised flight tests, center-of-gravity (CG) tuning, and payload station check. |
| **Phase 4: Round 1 (No Payload)** | 45 Mins | Unweighted distance & air-time evaluations (2 attempts per team). |
| **Phase 5: Round 2 (Nut Ladder)** | 1.25 Hours | Payload trials using standardized heavy hex nuts (2 attempts per team). |

## 🛠️ Technical Regulations & Rules

### 1. Geometric & Weight Constraints
- **Maximum Wingspan:** $50.0	ext{ cm}$
- **Maximum Fuselage Length:** $50.0	ext{ cm}$
- **Minimum/Maximum Aircraft Weight:** No restriction (unweighted aircraft).

*(Note: Non-compliant entries exceeding 50.0 cm in wingspan or length will be disqualified during technical inspection.)*

---

### 2. Materials & Fabrication

####  Approved Materials:
- Corrugated plastic sheets (2 sheets provided per team by the organizers).
- Cyanoacrylate (super glue), hot-melt adhesive, or PVA wood glue.
- Modeling clay or metal paperclips (**strictly reserved for CG ballast**).

####  Prohibited Materials:
- Active propulsion of any kind (electric motors, propellers, rubber bands, CO₂ cartridges).
- Pre-molded plastic components or commercial RC aircraft parts.
- Metal rods, lead weights, or liquid ballast.

##  Scoring Formulas

### Round 1: No-Payload Trials
Evaluates unweighted distance ($D$, in meters) and air time ($T$, in seconds):

$$S_1 = D + (2.5 	imes T) - P_{	ext{penalty}}$$

- Each team gets **2 attempts**; the higher score is kept.

---

### Round 2: Payload ("Nut Ladder") Trials
Evaluates payload capacity ($W_{	ext{payload}}$, mass of nuts carried in grams) and flight stability ($T$, air time in seconds):

$$S_2 = (W_{	ext{payload}} 	imes 1) + (T 	imes 3) - P_{	ext{penalty}}$$

- Standardized steel hex nuts are supplied by the committee.
- Nuts are attached one at a time at the marked payload station on the fuselage.
- If a nut detaches during flight, its mass is excluded, and a penalty is applied.
- Each team gets **2 attempts**; the higher score is kept.

---

### Final Score & Tie-Breaker
$$S_{	ext{final}} = S_1 + S_2$$

* **Tie-Breaker:** In case of equal $S_{	ext{final}}$, the team carrying higher total mass ($W_{	ext{payload}}$) in Round 2 wins.

##  Penalty Schedule

| Infraction | Penalty ($P_{	ext{penalty}}$) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Foot fault** at launch line | 2 Points |
| **Payload nut(s) detach** mid-flight | 8 Points |
| **Structural failure** in flight (e.g., wing snap) | 10 Points |
| **Exit beyond designated flight boundary** | Attempt Voided |

##  Key Aerodynamic Equations & Principles

1. **Static Longitudinal Stability Condition:**  
   $$X_{	ext{CG}} < X_{	ext{CP}}$$  
   *(The Center of Gravity must be strictly forward of the Center of Pressure).*

2. **Pitch Adjustments:**
   - **Nose-Up Stall / Loop:** Move CG forward (add nose ballast).
   - **Nose-Down Dive:** Lighten nose or add positive elevator trim to horizontal stabilizer.

3. **Dihedral Angle ($\Gamma$):**
   - Incorporate a wing sweep/angle of $\Gamma pprox 3^\circ - 5^\circ$ for passive roll stability against side drafts.
